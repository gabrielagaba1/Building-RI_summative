        About

        A small ledger for people whose money leaves in small amounts.

        Why it exists

        Student money rarely disappears in one big purchase. It goes a moto ride at a time, a brochette at a time, a bundle of airtime at a time, until the month ends early. Amafaranga makes those small amounts visible.

        Log what you spend, set a cap, and the note on the dashboard tells you the truth in your currency of choice. The name is Kinyarwanda for money.

        Nothing leaves your browser. No accounts, no servers, no tracking.
